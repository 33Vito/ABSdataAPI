## 1. Division of Statistical Responsibility in South Korea (BoK vs. KOSTAT/MODS)

### Takeaway
South Korea operates a distinctive decentralized, hybrid national statistical model under the Bank of Korea Act (Article 86) and the Statistics Act, where the Bank of Korea (BoK) directly compiles National Accounts (GDP, GNI, Input-Output tables, National Balance Sheets), Producer Price Index (PPI), Balance of Payments, and Money & Banking statistics, while Statistics Korea (KOSTAT)—elevated and renamed the **Ministry of Data and Statistics (MODS / 국가데이터처)** on October 1, 2025—compiles the Consumer Price Index (CPI), Economically Active Population Survey (unemployment), and Industrial Production. In early 2025, BoK split its Economic Statistics Department into two separate departments (**Economic Statistics Department 1** and **Economic Statistics Department 2**) for the first time in its history to strengthen SNA 2025 implementation and separate real-sector national accounts from financial, price, and enterprise statistics.

### Cited Findings
- **Statutory Basis for BoK Statistical Compilation:** The Bank of Korea compiles and disseminates national economic statistics pursuant to **Article 86 of the Bank of Korea Act** (`한국은행법 제86조`) and the **Statistics Act** (`통계법`), holding official designation for core macroeconomic statistics such as National Accounts (Designated Statistic No. 301009 / `지정통계 제301009호`) — [Bank of Korea Statistical Release Schedule](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/main/contents.do?menuNo=200998); [Ministry of Data and Statistics (MODS)](https://mods.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a20302010000).
- **2025 Split of BoK's Economic Statistics Department (Two-Department Structure):** Announced on December 26, 2024 and effective in the first half of 2025, BoK reorganized its single Economic Statistics Department into **Economic Statistics Department 1 (`경제통계1국`)** and **Economic Statistics Department 2 (`경제통계2국`)** to prepare for the **2025 System of National Accounts (SNA 2025)** and enhance consistency across real-sector statistics — [Edaily (Dec 26, 2024)](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/ReadE?newsId=02952006639122456).
  - **Economic Statistics Department 2 (`경제통계2국` — Real-Sector National Accounts):** Owns GDP, Input-Output tables (`투입산출통계`), National Balance Sheets (`국민대차대조표 / 국민B/S`), digital economy statistics, and satellite accounts. Comprises the National Income Coordination Team (`국민소득총괄팀`), Expenditure National Income Team (`지출국민소득팀`), Distribution National Income Team (`분배국민소득팀`), Input-Output Team (`투입산출팀`), National Balance Sheet Team (`국민B/S팀`), and a newly created National Accounts Planning Team (`국민계정기획팀`) — [Edaily (Dec 26, 2024)](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/ReadE?newsId=02952006639122456).
  - **Economic Statistics Department 1 (`경제통계1국` — Financial, Price, External & Enterprise Statistics):** Owns Monetary & Liquidity Aggregates (`통화지표`), Household Credit (`가계신용`), Flow of Funds (`자금순환`), Balance of Payments (`국제수지`), International Investment Position (`국외투자통계`), Price Statistics (PPI, Export/Import Price Indices), Financial Statement Analysis / Corporate Statistics (`기업통계`), and Economic Sentiment Indices (`경제심리지수`), as well as statistical development using structured/unstructured data and new analytical methods — [Edaily (Dec 26, 2024)](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/ReadE?newsId=02952006639122456); [IMF DSBB Korea PPI Metadata](https://dsbb.imf.org/sdds/dqaf-base/country/KOR/category/PPI00).
- **2025 Elevation of Statistics Korea (KOSTAT) to Ministry of Data and Statistics (MODS / 국가데이터처):** Following National Assembly passage of the Government Organization Act amendment on September 26, 2025 and Cabinet approval on September 30, 2025, Statistics Korea (previously an external agency under the Ministry of Economy and Finance since 1990) was officially upgraded and launched on **October 1, 2025** as the **Ministry of Data and Statistics (MODS / 국가데이터처)** under the Prime Minister's Office, with expanded authority for government-wide data governance, cross-agency data linkage, and AI-era statistical coordination — [MODS Official Launch Announcement (Oct 2025)](https://mods.go.kr/board.es?mid=a10301010000&bid=246&tag=&act=view&list_no=438851).
- **MODS (formerly KOSTAT) Core Macroeconomic Portfolio:** MODS compiles and publishes South Korea's **Consumer Price Index (CPI)** (monthly), **Economically Active Population Survey (EAPS)** (employment/unemployment), **Monthly Industrial Statistics / Index of Services** (industrial production and service sector output), **Household Income and Expenditure Survey**, **Economic Census**, **Mining and Manufacturing Survey**, **Construction Work Statistics**, and **Online Shopping Statistics** — [MODS Consumer Price Index Portal](https://mods.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a20209010000); [MODS IMF DSBB NSDP Hub](https://mods.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a20302010000); [IMF DSBB Korea CPI Metadata](https://dsbb.imf.org/sdds/dqaf-base/country/KOR/category/CPI00).
- **BoK Producer Price Index (PPI) Scope:** BoK's Economic Statistics Department (now Economic Statistics Department 1) directly compiles and releases the monthly PPI on an ex-factory price basis in accordance with the IMF Manual on Producer Price Indices, alongside Export and Import Price Indices — [IMF DSBB Korea PPI Metadata](https://dsbb.imf.org/sdds/dqaf-base/country/KOR/category/PPI00); [Bank of Korea News Release](https://www.bok.or.kr/eng/bbs/E0000634/view.do?nttId=10094096&oldMenuNo=400007&menuNo=400069&programType=newsDataEng&depth=400069&relate=Y).
- **Historical Context on CPI:** Consumer price compilation was historically transferred from BoK to Statistics Korea decades ago, establishing the current split where MODS/KOSTAT owns consumer prices and household/labor/industrial surveys while BoK owns producer/trade prices, national accounts, balance of payments, and monetary/financial accounts — [MODS CPI Portal](https://mods.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a20209010000).

### Inferences
- Because South Korea assigns GDP/National Accounts and PPI to the central bank while assigning CPI, labor, and industrial production to the national statistical office (now MODS), BoK plays a dual role rarely seen in G20 peers: it is simultaneously a primary *producer* of real-sector national accounts and a heavy *consumer* of external survey and administrative source data from MODS and line ministries.
- Elevating KOSTAT to MODS under the Prime Minister's Office in October 2025 removes MODS from MOEF's formal hierarchy, potentially reinforcing statistical independence while expanding cross-ministerial administrative data sharing pipelines that feed BoK's national accounts.

### Gaps
- Exact post-October 2025 updates to bilateral inter-agency working-level committees between MODS and BoK's newly split Economic Statistics Departments 1 and 2 have not yet been published in detail in English-language BoK annual reports as of early 2026.

## 2. How BoK Compiles GDP vs. Ingests CPI and External Statistics from KOSTAT/MODS and Ministries

### Takeaway
BoK's ingestion architecture sharply differentiates between **(a) compiling National Accounts (GDP)**—which requires ingesting raw administrative microdata and foundational economic surveys from MODS (formerly KOSTAT), the National Tax Service (NTS), Korea Customs Service (KCS), and direct BoK electronic surveys via dedicated collection systems like **DACOS** under Statistics Act administrative-data provisions and inter-agency MoUs—and **(b) ingesting finished headline indicators like CPI from MODS/KOSTAT** for monetary policy monitoring and re-dissemination in ECOS, alongside international **SDMX / IMF SDDS Plus** dissemination via Korea's National Summary Data Page (NSDP).

### Cited Findings
- **Compiling GDP as a Derived/Processed Statistic (`가공통계`) from Multi-Agency Inputs:** BoK compiles GDP (quarterly advance/preliminary estimates and annual preliminary/final estimates across production, expenditure, and income approaches) by integrating foundational surveys and administrative records from external agencies rather than conducting stand-alone economy-wide censuses — [BoK National Accounts Lecture (2020 Benchmark Revision, July 2024)](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000217/view.do?nttId=10086131&menuNo=200144); [BoK National Accounts Overview](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000217/view.do?nttId=11063016&oldMenuNo=201151&menuNo=200144&programType=multiCont&depth=200144&relate=Y).
  - **Inputs from MODS / Statistics Korea:** Industrial production (`광공업동향조사`), Index of Services (`서비스업동향조사`), Economic Census (`경제총조사`), Mining & Manufacturing Survey, Construction Survey, Wholesale & Retail Trade Survey, Household Income & Expenditure Survey (`가계동향조사`), Economically Active Population Survey, and **CPI** (used both as a price deflator for private consumption and as a monetary policy target indicator) — [BoK 2020 Benchmark Revision (July 2024)](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000217/view.do?nttId=10086131&menuNo=200144); [MODS NSDP Portal](https://mods.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a20302010000).
  - **Administrative Tax Data from National Tax Service (NTS / 국세청):** Corporate income tax returns (`법인세`), Value-Added Tax filings (`부가가치세`), and individual income tax filings (`소득세`) are ingested by BoK—particularly for annual final GDP estimates and 5-year benchmark revisions—to measure corporate operating surplus, compensation of employees, small/micro-enterprise activity, and parts of the non-observed economy not captured in sample surveys. In BoK's **2020 benchmark revision (released June 5, 2024)**, expanded linkage of Economic Census data with NTS administrative tax data raised South Korea's 2022 nominal GDP by more than KRW 165 trillion (and lifted 2023 per-capita GDP by 7.2%) — [BoK 2020 Benchmark Revision (July 2024)](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000217/view.do?nttId=10086131&menuNo=200144); [Yonhap Infomax (June 2024)](https://news.einfomax.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4312214).
  - **Customs Administrative Data from Korea Customs Service (KCS / 관세청):** Merchandise customs clearance records (trade values, unit values, quantities by HS item) feed directly into GDP expenditure-side exports/imports, real trading gains/losses (GNI), and Balance of Payments (aligned with BPM6 adjustments for goods for processing and merchanting) — [BIS IFC Working Paper No. 12 on BoP Statistics](https://www.bis.org/publications/ifc-working-paper-12-balance-payments-statistics.pdf); [BoK National Accounts Overview](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000217/view.do?nttId=11063016&oldMenuNo=201151&menuNo=200144&programType=multiCont&depth=200144&relate=Y).
  - **BoK Direct Primary Collection Systems (for PPI, I-O, BSI/CSI, Financial Stats):** For statistics BoK collects directly from firms and financial institutions, BoK operates dedicated external collection portals integrated with ECOS: **DACOS (`통계자료입수시스템` — Data Collection System)**, the **Price Survey System (`물가조사시스템`)**, the **Electronic Survey System (`전자설문시스템`)**, and the **Input-Output Electronic Survey System (`산업연관표 전자조사시스템`)** — [Digital Daily (Jan 22, 2020)](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2020012208565629065).
- **Legal Authority & Inter-Agency MoUs for Administrative Data Sharing:** Under the **Statistics Act** (Article 24 / Article 32 administrative-data provision framework), designated statistical agencies including BoK are legally empowered to request administrative records from tax, customs, and other government agencies under strict confidentiality safeguards to reduce respondent burden and improve accuracy; BoK and KOSTAT/MODS have also signed formal **Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs)** covering foundational statistical data exchange, national accounts accuracy improvement, and joint methodological research — [Bank of Korea Digital Archive (BoK–KOSTAT MoU)](https://archives.bok.or.kr/archive/search/file/103636); [Korean National Law Information Center (Statistics Act)](http://www.law.go.kr/LSW//lsLawLinkInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=900542691&chrClsCd=010202).
- **KOSIS Open API & SDMX Infrastructure (External Finished Statistics):** MODS/KOSTAT operates the **KOSIS (Korean Statistical Information Service) Open API** (`kosis.kr/openapi`), which serves official national statistics across all Korean statistical agencies—including CPI, employment, industrial production, and re-hosted BoK series—in **JSON, SDMX (DSD, Generic, StructureSpecific XML), XLS, and XML** formats, subject to public rate limits of **200 requests per minute** and **40,000 cells per request** — [KOSIS Open API Guide](https://kosis.kr/serviceInfo/openAPIGuide.do).
- **IMF SDDS Plus, NSDP, and SDMX Exchange:** South Korea adheres to the IMF's highest-tier **SDDS Plus** standard. MODS hosts Korea's **National Summary Data Page (NSDP)** linking BoK and MODS macroeconomic indicators (National Accounts/GDP, PPI, CPI, Balance of Payments, Financial Sector), while IMF SDDS Plus mandates machine-readable **SDMX-ML / SDMX REST** data structures linked to the NSDP — [MODS IMF DSBB NSDP Page](https://mods.go.kr/menu.es?mid=a20302010000); [IMF DSBB SDMX Data Structures for NSDP](https://dsbb.imf.org/sdds-plus/page/SDMXDataStructuresforNSDP). BoK also operates an internal **International Organization Statistics Provision System (`국제기구 통계제공시스템`)** within ECOS to automate outbound reporting to international organizations (IMF, BIS, OECD) — [Digital Daily (Jan 22, 2020)](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2020012208565629065).

### Inferences
- Quarterly advance/preliminary GDP relies heavily on timely high-frequency monthly/quarterly indicators (MODS industrial/service output, CPI, KCS trade clearances, financial/payment indicators) to meet fast release deadlines, whereas annual final GDP and 5-year benchmark revisions heavily ingest comprehensive administrative microdata (NTS corporate/VAT/income tax returns and Economic Census linkage) via formal inter-agency secure data transfers rather than public APIs.
- While KOSIS exposes finished CPI and other MODS statistics via public JSON/SDMX APIs, BoK's internal ingestion of administrative tax and customs microdata for GDP compilation operates over privileged inter-agency government channels governed by Statistics Act confidentiality rules, completely separate from public Open APIs.

### Gaps
- **Exact technical transport protocol for raw administrative microdata:** Public BoK and MODS documents confirm legal/MoU-based receipt of NTS tax filings and KCS customs data, as well as BoK's DACOS ingestion portal, but do not publicly disclose the exact low-level file transfer mechanism (e.g., government intranet GPKI/SFTP batch, dedicated leased line, or internal API) or exact delivery lag schedules between NTS/KCS servers and BoK's internal network.
- **Exact automated ingestion method for MODS CPI into ECOS:** While ECOS explicitly re-disseminates MODS/KOSTAT series like CPI (with documented publication lags relative to the primary agency), BoK does not publicly document whether its internal ECOS backend pulls MODS CPI via KOSIS's SDMX/Open API, a dedicated inter-agency batch feed, or semi-automated ingestion via DACOS.

## 3. Architecture and Operation of BoK's ECOS (Economic Statistics System) and Open API

### Takeaway
BoK's **Economic Statistics System (`ECOS`, ecos.bok.or.kr)** is not merely a public web portal; it is an end-to-end statistical production, storage, and dissemination suite split into internal production/microdata/international-reporting subsystems and external portal/Open API/collection (`DACOS`) subsystems. Originally built in 2004 on SAS and Excel, BoK launched a completely rebuilt **New ECOS (`신ECOS`)** on **May 30, 2022** (built with Bankware Global and Samsung SDS Brightics AI), modernizing internal compilation pipelines, halving public categories from 18 to 9, upgrading its RESTful JSON/XML **ECOS Open API**, and continuing to re-disseminate external agency series (including MODS/KOSTAT CPI) alongside BoK's own statistics under distinct copyright/attribution terms.

### Cited Findings
- **End-to-End ECOS Architecture (Internal vs. External Subsystems):** ECOS supports the full statistical lifecycle—source data ingestion, compilation, validation, migration, internal analysis, and external dissemination—structured into two major tiers — [Digital Daily (Jan 22, 2020)](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2020012208565629065):
  - **Internal Subsystems:** (1) **Statistical Production System (`통계생산시스템`)** for data ingestion, compilation, transfer, and quality/error validation; (2) **Micro/Source Data Service System (`기초자료서비스시스템`, MDSS)**; and (3) **International Organization Statistics Provision System (`국제기구 통계제공시스템`)** — [Digital Daily (Jan 22, 2020)](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2020012208565629065).
  - **External Subsystems:** (1) **ECOS Public Web Portal (`ecos.bok.or.kr`)**, Mobile Service, and **ECOS Open API (`ecos.bok.or.kr/api/`)**; (2) **DACOS (`통계자료입수시스템`)** for collecting raw statistical submissions; (3) **Price Survey System (`물가조사시스템`)**; (4) **Electronic Survey System (`전자설문시스템`)**; and (5) **Input-Output Table Electronic Survey System (`산업연관표 전자조사시스템`)** — [Digital Daily (Jan 22, 2020)](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2020012208565629065); [ECOS Portal](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/).
- **May 2022 Full Rebuild (`신ECOS` / New ECOS):**
  - After 16–18 years running on a legacy 2004 architecture reliant on desktop **SAS and Excel**, BoK completed Stage 1 consulting in 2019, awarded the 2-year-3-month Stage 2 rebuild contract in April 2020 to **Bankware Global** incorporating **Samsung SDS Brightics AI** (data analytics platform), and officially launched **New ECOS on May 30, 2022** — [Hankyoreh (May 30, 2022)](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/economy/economy_general/1044968.html); [Joongang News / Ejanews (July 29, 2022)](https://www.ejanews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=306653); [Digital Daily (Jan 22, 2020)](https://m.ddaily.co.kr/page/view/2020012208565629065).
  - The 2022 rebuild eliminated legacy desktop Excel/SAS compilation silos by **redeveloping Monetary & Financial Statistics and PPI** and **newly systemizing Balance of Payments, Flow of Funds, and Financial Institution Weighted Average Interest Rates** onto a common platform featuring automated error verification, visual compilation workflows, and automated statistical table/report generation — [Hankyoreh (May 30, 2022)](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/economy/economy_general/1044968.html).
  - Public statistical taxonomy was consolidated from **18 major categories down to 9 major categories** to shorten navigation depth, accompanied by an upgraded **Open API** rollout in June 2022 — [Hankyoreh (May 30, 2022)](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/economy/economy_general/1044968.html).
- **ECOS Open API Technical Format & Access:** Operated by BoK's Economic Statistics Department 1 (Statistical Information Team), `https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/` offers free RESTful access (after API key registration) returning **JSON or XML** across endpoints such as `StatisticSearch/{key}/{format}/{lang}/{start}/{end}/{stat_code}/{cycle}/{start_date}/{end_date}/{item_code}` (alongside metadata/table listing and Key 100 Indicators endpoints). Unlike KOSIS Open API, which natively serves full SDMX (DSD/Generic/StructureSpecific), ECOS Open API focuses on custom REST JSON/XML payloads, while the ECOS web portal supports interactive multi-dimensional queries and Excel/CSV downloads — [ECOS Open API Portal](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/); [KOSIS Open API Guide](https://kosis.kr/serviceInfo/openAPIGuide.do).
- **Re-Dissemination of External Agency Statistics (e.g., KOSTAT/MODS CPI) in ECOS:**
  - ECOS intentionally hosts and re-disseminates key macroeconomic indicators produced by external domestic agencies (such as MODS/KOSTAT's Consumer Price Index) alongside BoK's own statistics so users have a single macro hub — [ECOS Portal](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/).
  - **Publication Lag, Disclaimer, and Dual Copyright Regime:** BoK explicitly notes on ECOS that external-agency statistics may exhibit **update/publication lags** relative to the primary agency's release schedule and disclaims warranty over their accuracy or timeliness. While BoK-authored statistics are free for both commercial and non-commercial use with attribution, **copyright for third-party statistics in ECOS (such as CPI) remains with the originating agency (MODS/KOSTAT)**: non-commercial use with dual attribution is permitted, whereas commercial redistribution or paid database integration requires prior approval from the originating agency — [ECOS Portal](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/).

### Inferences
- The explicit mention of update lags and dual-copyright disclaimers for third-party series in ECOS indicates that ECOS acts as a convenience aggregator for external indicators like CPI rather than the real-time authoritative source of record; external quantitative users seeking zero-lag CPI releases still query MODS/KOSIS directly.
- The 2022 New ECOS migration confirms that even a technologically advanced central bank relied substantially on desktop **Excel and SAS** for core series (such as Balance of Payments and Flow of Funds) well into 2020–2022 before migrating them to a unified analytics platform.

### Gaps
- Exact internal API/ETL job architecture connecting external agencies (MODS/KOSTAT, Korea Exchange, MOEF, etc.) to ECOS's third-party tables—specifically whether updates are triggered automatically via KOSIS Open API polling, automated batch files, or manual staff upload—is not disclosed in public BoK technical documentation.

## 4. BoK Modernization, BIS IFC Research, Alternative/Big Data, and Commercial Data Vendors

### Takeaway
Between 2022 and 2026, BoK executed a multi-layered data modernization roadmap progressing from **New ECOS (May 2022)** to an enterprise data platform (**BIDAS — BOK Integrated Data Analytics System**) and culminating in the **January 21, 2026** launch of **BOKI (`Bank Of Korea Intelligence`)**, the world's first sovereign on-premise generative AI platform deployed inside a central bank's air-gapped internal network (built with Naver Cloud / HyperCLOVA X), featuring `BIDAS.ai` for natural-language data retrieval and analysis. Across BIS IFC papers and BoK working papers, BoK actively uses administrative microdata, credit card/alternative big data, and machine learning for nowcasting private consumption, experimental sentiment indices, household debt micro-panels, and real-time payment liquidity monitoring, while relying on commercial global vendors (**Bloomberg, CEIC, Haver Analytics, Refinitiv/LSEG Datastream, Macrobond**) for foreign/international macroeconomic data.

### Cited Findings
- **BIDAS & BOKI Sovereign AI Platform (Launched January 21, 2026):**
  - On **January 21, 2026**, at the joint BoK–Naver AX Conference, BoK unveiled **BOKI (`Bank Of Korea Intelligence`)**, described as the **first sovereign generative AI platform built directly inside a central bank's air-gapped internal (on-premise) network**, developed over 1.5 years starting in 2024 in partnership with Naver / Naver Cloud (using Neurocloud for HyperCLOVA X) alongside a public-sector network security pilot with the National Intelligence Service — [Yonhap Infomax (Jan 21, 2026)](https://news.einfomax.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4394606); [Edaily (Jan 21, 2026)](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/ReadE?newsId=03870406645320016).
  - BOKI integrates directly with BoK's enterprise standardized data catalog and analytics platform, **BIDAS (`BOK Integrated Data Analytics System` / 종합데이터플랫폼)**, via **`BIDAS.ai`**, enabling BoK staff to search standardized internal/external data assets and generate analytical workflows using natural language without coding — [Yonhap Infomax (Jan 21, 2026)](https://news.einfomax.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=4394606); [Edaily (Jan 21, 2026)](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/ReadE?newsId=03870406645320016); [Seoul Economic Daily (Jan 2026)](https://www.sedaily.com/article/14173850).
  - BOKI's five pillars are `BOKI.ra` (Research Assistant), `BOKI.ca` (Compliance Assistant), `BOKI.da` (Document Assistant), `BIDAS.ai` (Data Analysis linked to BIDAS), and `BOKI.tr` (Multilingual Translator for BoK publications) — [Edaily (Jan 21, 2026)](https://marketin.edaily.co.kr/News/ReadE?newsId=03870406645320016).
- **Credit Card, Big Data, and Machine Learning for GDP/Consumption Nowcasting & Sentiment:**
  - BoK's Economic Statistics Department (Statistical Research Team / `통계연구반`) published **BOK Working Paper No. 2022-16** (*"Nowcasting Private Consumption with Big Data"* / `빅데이터를 이용한 실시간 민간소비 예측`, Sept 26, 2022, Seungjoon Shin & Beomseok Seo), demonstrating that high-frequency big data combined with machine-learning variable selection significantly improved real-time private consumption nowcasting accuracy, especially during high-uncertainty periods like COVID-19 — [BOK Economic Research No. 2022-16](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/P0002455/view.do?menuNo=500535&nttId=10072855).
  - BoK's Statistical Research series (`통계연구`, hosted by Economic Statistics Department 1) documents active development and operationalization of alternative/big data and AI in statistical compilation, including: *"Status of Domestic and Foreign Statistical Compilation Using AI"* (National Accounts Review 2021 No. 2), *"Compilation and Use of the News Sentiment Index (NSI) Using Machine Learning"* (BoK's experimental weekly/monthly ML-based sentiment indicator), *"Usefulness and Future Challenges of Credit Card Data"*, and studies utilizing the **BOK Household Debt DB** — [BoK Statistical Research Portal](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000233/view.do?menuNo=200707&nttId=10070675).
  - Credit card transaction data is also utilized alongside administrative records in monitoring near-term private consumption and enhancing consistency around GDP benchmark revisions — [BoK 2020 Benchmark Revision (July 2024)](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/B0000217/view.do?nttId=10086131&menuNo=200144).
- **BIS Irving Fisher Committee (IFC) Publications by Bank of Korea Staff:**
  - **Big Data & Real-Time Payment Analytics:** *IFC Bulletin No. 50 ("The use of big data analytics and artificial intelligence in central banking")* documents BoK's application of big data and network analytics (including collaboration with FNA) to monitor real-time intraday liquidity and early-warning risks on Korea's large-value payment system, **BoK-Wire+** — [BIS IFC Bulletin No. 50](https://www.bis.org/publications/ifc-bulletin-50-use-big-data-analytics-and-artificial-intelligence-central-banking.pdf).
  - **Microdata Panels (Consumer Credit / Household Debt):** MiRa Kim (Bank of Korea), *"BOK Consumer Credit Panel"* (9th Biennial IFC Conference, Aug 2018), details BoK's construction of a granular micro-level consumer credit panel (combining credit bureau/financial institution records) to bridge macroprudential data gaps — [BIS IFC Conference Paper (Kim, 2018)](https://www.bis.org/events/20180830/ninth-biennial-ifc-conference-are-post-crisis-statistical-initiatives-completed/kim.pdf).
  - **Administrative Data & BPM6 External Accounts:** *IFC Working Paper No. 12 ("Balance of payments statistics")* includes BoK technical contributions on integrating customs administrative data and firm-level data to measure goods for processing and merchanting under BPM6 — [BIS IFC Working Paper No. 12](https://www.bis.org/publications/ifc-working-paper-12-balance-payments-statistics.pdf).
  - **G20 Data Gaps Initiative (DGI-3) & Exploratory Micro-Statistics (2026):** Soohoon Choi (Bank of Korea Economic Statistics), *"Pilot Compilation Results for Climate Finance Debt Securities"* (BIS/IFC, Aug 2026), and *IFC Bulletin No. 68 Overview* highlight BoK's use of security-by-security / micro-level administrative and market registries to compile exploratory climate-finance statistics without imposing new broad surveys — [BIS IFC Paper (Choi, Aug 2026)](https://www.bis.org/2026-08/3.3_choi.pdf); [BIS IFC Bulletin No. 68 Overview (Aug 2026)](https://www.bis.org/2026-08/ifc-bulletin-68_overview.pdf).
- **Commercial Data Vendors for Foreign/International vs. Domestic Macro Data:**
  - For **domestic macro statistics**, BoK relies primarily on its own compilation (ECOS/DACOS/MDSS) and direct domestic agency sources (MODS/KOSIS, NTS, KCS, MOEF, domestic financial institutions).
  - For **foreign macroeconomic indicators, global market consensus, and cross-country comparisons**—for example in BoK's *Overseas Economic Focus* (`해외경제 포커스`), *Economic Outlook*, and international research—BoK publications and Korean policy research institutions regularly cite **Bloomberg** (real-time global markets, yields, FX, commodities, consensus forecasts), **CEIC** (China, emerging-market, and advanced-economy official macro time series), **Haver Analytics**, **Refinitiv/LSEG Datastream**, and **Macrobond**, alongside international organizations (IMF, OECD, BIS) — [Korea Institute for International Economic Policy (citing BoK Overseas Economic Focus / international sources)](https://www.kiep.go.kr/); [Korea Capital Market Institute (citing CEIC/BoK)](https://www.kcmi.re.kr/). (Note: Search on BoK's public competitive-bidding disclosure portal for English vendor names `Bloomberg`, `Haver`, `CEIC`, `Macrobond`, `LSEG`, `Refinitiv` returned 0 public competitive-bid records, likely due to sole-source contracts (`수의계약`), local reseller names, or procurement thresholds.)

### Inferences
- BoK's architecture shows a clear three-layer internal evolution: (1) **DACOS + New ECOS (2022)** for structured official statistical production and public dissemination; (2) **BIDAS** as an enterprise-wide standardized data lake/catalog spanning official, alternative, and external research datasets; and (3) **BOKI / `BIDAS.ai` (Jan 2026)** as an air-gapped sovereign AI query and econometric-assisted research layer sitting atop BIDAS.
- Unlike central banks that deploy public cloud SaaS for analytics, BoK strictly requires air-gapped on-premise infrastructure (Naver Neurocloud on-prem) to protect sensitive pre-release statistics, tax/credit microdata, and monetary policy deliberations.

### Gaps
- **Commercial vendor procurement details and internal data pipelines:** Exact contract values, seat counts, and technical ingestion connectors (e.g., whether Bloomberg Data License / Haver DLX / CEIC API / Macrobond feeds are automatically ingested into BIDAS) are not publicly disclosed in BoK's public procurement registry or annual reports.
- **Exact scope of `BOK-D` naming:** Public Korean sources up to 2026 refer to BoK's enterprise internal data analytics platform as **BIDAS** (`BOK Integrated Data Analytics System`) and its sovereign AI layer as **BOKI** (`BIDAS.ai`), rather than `BOK-D`; no authoritative public BoK source confirmed a separate system officially named `BOK-D`.

## 5. Pre-Release and Embargo Arrangements Between KOSTAT/MODS, BoK, and Government Ministries

### Takeaway
There is a notable **documented gap between IMF DSBB's static metadata and South Korea's actual domestic statutory practice**. While IMF DSBB's South Korea CPI metadata states that *"no one outside Statistics Korea has access to the data before their release to the public,"* South Korea's **Statistics Act Article 27-2** permits formal, logged pre-release (`사전제공`) to relevant policy agencies upon written request for official policy duties—and a 2023 National Assembly audit revealed that KOSTAT granted **620 pre-releases across 33 agencies** over a ~16-month period, including **54 pre-releases to the Bank of Korea** and **71 pre-releases of CPI** (primarily to MOEF, the Presidential Office, and BoK). Conversely, BoK also provides logged statutory pre-releases of its own indicators (National Accounts, BoP, PPI/Trade indices) to relevant ministries such as MOEF and publishes an annual public log under Statistics Act Article 27-2(5).

### Cited Findings
- **IMF DSBB Official Metadata Statement (CPI Simultaneous Release):** Korea's IMF DSBB DQAF metadata for the Consumer Price Index explicitly states that **"No one outside Statistics Korea has access to the data before their release to the public"** and that data are released simultaneously to all interested parties via press release on the agency's website — [IMF DSBB Korea CPI Metadata](https://dsbb.imf.org/sdds/dqaf-base/country/KOR/category/CPI00).
- **Contradicting / Nuancing Domestic Legal Framework (`통계법 제27조의2`):** Under **Article 27-2 of South Korea's Statistics Act** (*Prohibition on exerting influence, leaking, or unauthorized use in statistical compilation and dissemination*), pre-release of official statistics is prohibited *in principle*, **except** under **Article 27-2(2)(2)(b)** (`제27조의2 제2항 제2호 나목`) when a relevant government/public institution formally requests pre-release as necessary to perform its statutory policy duties (such as monetary policy, inflation stabilization, or fiscal management), subject to strict timing windows (e.g., after 12:00 noon on the day prior to release under implementing rules), written requests, and mandatory pre-release registry logs (`사전제공 관리대장`) — [Korean National Law Information Center (Statistics Act Art. 27-2)](http://www.law.go.kr/LSW//lsLawLinkInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=900542691&chrClsCd=010202); [MODS Pre-Release Regulation/Status Filing](https://www.mods.go.kr/boardDownload.es?bid=1601&list_no=435774&seq=2).
- **Empirical Evidence of KOSTAT-to-BoK and KOSTAT-to-MOEF Pre-Releases (2023 Parliamentary Audit):** According to KOSTAT's official pre-release log (`통계청 사전제공 현황`) submitted to the National Assembly Strategy and Finance Committee audit in October 2023 (covering May 10, 2022 – August 30, 2023):
  - KOSTAT executed **620 pre-releases** across **33 institutions** (averaging ~39 per month).
  - Top recipient agencies were: **Ministry of Economy and Finance (MOEF) — 117 times**, **Office of the President — 111 times**, **Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs — 99 times**, **Office for Government Policy Coordination — 74 times**, and **Bank of Korea — 54 times** — [JoongAng Ilbo (Oct 12, 2023)](https://www.joongang.co.kr/article/25198803).
  - Major pre-released datasets included the **Economically Active Population Survey (105 times)**, **Industrial Activity Trends (85 times)**, **Consumer Price Index Survey (71 times)**, and **Household Income and Expenditure Survey (41 times)** — [JoongAng Ilbo (Oct 12, 2023)](https://www.joongang.co.kr/article/25198803).
- **BoK's Own Pre-Release Governance and Public Disclosure:** As a designated statistical agency under the Statistics Act, BoK announces its fixed annual statistical release calendar every December for the following year — [BoK Statistical Release Calendar](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/main/contents.do?menuNo=200998) — and publishes annual public disclosures of **`사전제공통계 및 변경 현황`** (*Pre-Released Statistics and Changes*) pursuant to **Article 27-2(5) of the Statistics Act**, covering pre-release access to BoK-compiled series including National Accounts (GDP), Balance of Payments, Trade Indices, and Flow of Funds — [BoK Notice: Pre-Released Statistics & Changes (nttId=10076503)](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/bbs/P0000561/view.do?nttId=10076503&menuNo=200037).
- **Press Embargo Practice:** Alongside statutory inter-agency pre-release, major market-sensitive releases (CPI, PPI, GDP) are distributed under strict press-corps **embargo** (`엠바고`) rules prior to the official release timestamp to enable accurate reporting, with sanctions (such as press-corps suspension) for embargo breaches — [BoK Statistical Release Policy](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/main/contents.do?menuNo=200998).

### Inferences
- The discrepancy between IMF DSBB's "no external pre-release access" text for Korean CPI and the 2023 parliamentary audit data (71 CPI pre-releases; 54 total pre-releases to BoK) illustrates why cross-checking domestic statutory disclosures (`사전제공 현황`) against international metadata (IMF DSBB) is critical: Korea's pre-release regime is legally codified, audited, and publicly logged under Statistics Act Article 27-2, yet not always reflected accurately in legacy IMF DSBB metadata descriptions.
- Following the 2023–2024 Board of Audit and Inspection (BAI) scrutiny over prior statistical-manipulation allegations and pre-release practices, both KOSTAT/MODS and BoK have emphasized strict compliance with written request procedures, timing restrictions (e.g., post-noon day-before windows), and annual public disclosure logs.

### Gaps
- **Exact 2025–2026 itemized breakdown of BoK's 54+ pre-releases by indicator and exact lead time (hours):** While the October 2023 JoongAng Ilbo parliamentary-audit report confirms BoK received 54 pre-releases from KOSTAT across 16 months (and that CPI was pre-released 71 times across recipient agencies), the exact per-indicator count specifically delivered to BoK (e.g., how many of those 54 were CPI vs. EAPS vs. Industrial Production) and the exact PDF contents of BoK's 2024–2025 `사전제공통계 및 변경 현황` attachments were not directly parsed in this research pass.
- **Post-October 2025 MODS pre-release rule revisions:** Whether the October 2025 elevation of KOSTAT to MODS under the Prime Minister's Office resulted in tighter or revised pre-release timing rules for MOEF and BoK in 2026 could not be verified from public announcements.
